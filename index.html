Our Features

Student Management

Manage student information in one place.Our Student Management feature helps the hostel administrator maintain complete student records in one place. The administrator can register new students by entering their name, mobile number, course, room number, and admission date. Student information can be viewed, searched, updated, and deleted whenever required. This feature reduces paperwork and makes student record management easy, fast, and organized.

Room Management

View room capacity and availability. The Room Management feature helps administrators manage hostel rooms efficiently. It stores room numbers, room capacity, monthly rent, and room availability details. Administrators can view room information and assign students to suitable rooms. This feature helps prevent confusion during room allocation and supports better utilization of hostel space.

Fee Management

Track hostel fee payment status.The Fees Management feature helps maintain student hostel fee records. The administrator can enter fee amounts, payment dates, and payment statuses such as Paid or Pending. The system displays collected fee totals and helps identify outstanding payments. This feature makes fee tracking more organized and reduces errors in manual calculations. Payment status should be verified before being marked as paid.
